What is OpenBH2002?

OpenBH2002 is an open-source engine that reimplements Beach Head 2002, the 2001 Windows shooter, so it runs natively in modern browsers with WebGPU or WebGL2. It plays the original levels, art and sounds. Choose the faithful 2001 look or a remaster with modern lighting and shadows.

Controls

Desktop: move the mouse to aim and hold the left button to fire. Space switches weapon, M fires a missile, F drops a flare at night, B calls a bomber strike, P pauses and Esc opens the menu. Shift+T switches between the faithful and remastered look.

Phones and tablets: drag anywhere to aim and hold the FIRE button to shoot. You can aim with one finger while firing with the other. Tap the buttons next to FIRE to switch weapon, launch missiles, drop flares or call the bombers.

Tips

  • Shoot parachute crates to collect health, bullets, cannon shells and missiles.
  • Use the cannon on tanks and the machine gun on soldiers and helicopters.
  • At night, drop a flare to light up the battlefield.
  • Save the bomber strike for when soldiers get close.
